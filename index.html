🏛 Aegis of Athens

A Peloponnesian War survival & strategy game

The year is 431 BC. Sparta marches on Attica. As Athens' last scholar-steward, you must feed the city and hold the walls.

  • Tap olive groves 🫒, vineyards 🍇 and fishing docks 🐟 to gather resources.
  • Your Press, Winery & Granary turn them into oil, wine and food.
  • Tap the Agora 🏛 (or hit Sell) to trade oil & wine for drachmas.
  • Spend drachmas to upgrade walls and hire hoplites ⚔ & archers 🏹.
  • Keep food stocked — a hungry army deserts!
  • Survive 12 Spartan assaults to save the polis.
